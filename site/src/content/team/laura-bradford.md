Laura’s interest in health and wellness has been lifelong, from playing in multiple sporting teams to regular gym and aerobics classes. She has always prioritised physical wellbeing.

Her path into physiotherapy began with a back injury of her own, through which she discovered physiotherapy and pilates. Inspired, she completed a Diploma in Pilates and went on to teach clinical pilates alongside a team of physiotherapists. Building on this foundation, she completed a Bachelor’s degree in Physiotherapy, graduating with distinction. Laura has completed additional training with both the WHTA and APA, and holds Level 1 Women’s Pelvic Health certification.

Laura’s background in exercise prescription shapes her approach to physiotherapy and helps her tailor treatment to each person. She has worked with a diverse range of clients, including peri-natal women, people facing cancer, people managing chronic back pain, athletes recovering from sporting injuries, people in pre- and post-surgery rehabilitation, and people with vestibular dysfunction (dizziness, vertigo and balance problems). Her own experience of the peri-natal journey gives her valuable insight that helps her connect with and support clients, and she brings empathy and care to understanding each person’s needs.

Beyond work, Laura loves spending time with her husband Zac and caring for their son Theodore. She also enjoys running and baking delicious cookies.
