Nicole has been professionally involved in the health and fitness industry since 2014. She began her own rehabilitation journey while training at The Australian Ballet School from 2009 to 2012. That extensive and comprehensive ballet training from a young age made her highly aware of human physiology and the benefits of exercise rehabilitation.

She has since graduated with a Bachelor of Physiotherapy with distinction (2021), a Bachelor of Clinical Exercise Physiology (2016) and a Diploma of Pilates Movement Therapy (2016). She has completed additional training with both the WHTA and APA, and holds Level 1 & 2 Women’s Pelvic Health certification.

Nicole has worked in both the public and private sectors across sport, pelvic health and musculoskeletal care. Her passion is using exercise as medicine to support people through their journey of health and ageing. She tailors and adapts each treatment plan to the needs of the person in front of her, whatever their exercise experience, abilities, pre-existing injuries or health concerns.

Nicole brings a vibrant and enthusiastic approach to instructing and educating. No two exercise programs are the same: she individualises and personalises each one to fit each patient’s health objectives and goals.

When Nicole isn’t at work, she enjoys getting outdoors, trying new restaurants and relaxing river walks with her pup, Jeff.
