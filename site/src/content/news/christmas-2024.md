Dear valued clients,

We hope this newsletter finds you in good health and high spirits! As we rapidly approach Christmas and the New Year, we have some updates to share with you.

## A heartfelt thank you

First of all, as we reflect on the past year, we want to extend our heartfelt gratitude for your ongoing support of our small business. We sincerely appreciate you choosing Embrace Movement Clinic and continuing to refer family and friends to us. It means so much to us that you choose to support a small business and trust us with your healthcare needs. Your trust and loyalty are truly appreciated!

## Group exercise rehabilitation classes update

Our group exercise rehabilitation classes are gaining momentum and getting busier! These classes are designed to support your recovery and overall strength and wellness in a supportive environment. We encourage you to secure your preferred ongoing timeslot for 2025. By letting us know, we can prebook you in from December.

## Socks and footwear

When participating in group exercise classes, we ask that you please wear pilates socks for hygiene reasons, as well as to reduce wear on the pilates equipment. We will have more stock available for 2025. We understand that at times enclosed footwear with orthotics needs to be worn when exercising, and we are happy to support this.

## Class packages

Class packages are available, and you can still claim on your health fund if you purchase a package.

## Important dates

The last day the clinic is open for 2024 is Friday, December 20th, as we take a break for Christmas. We will resume normal operations on Monday, January 6th. We look forward to seeing you in 2025.

We wish you a Merry Christmas and a Happy and Healthy New Year,

**Nicole, Laura and Jeff.**
