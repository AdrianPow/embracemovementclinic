## New equipment in the studio!

As many of you have noticed, we’ve said goodbye to our very first piece of equipment and welcomed a BRAND NEW reformer and reformer-trapeze combo!

To help keep everything fresh, clean and long-lasting, we kindly ask:

- Preferably NO sneakers on the new equipment.
- Grip socks are preferred for hygiene and longevity. (Forgot yours? No worries: grip socks are available in the studio for $15, or feel free to bring your hospital or trampoline socks.)
- Need orthotics during your session? Sneakers are fine; just let us know so we can adjust the setup for you.
- There is a shoe rack outside to minimise the dirt coming in.

## Online bookings

Want to save time and avoid creating new profiles every time you book? Ask Nicole or Laura for your unique online booking code for quicker booking.

## AI tools trial

We’re trialling healthcare-specific AI tools to help streamline admin and enhance your care. These are used for note efficiency, sending summary emails, and drafting documents and letters.

No private or identifying info is shared externally, and everything is reviewed by your practitioner. The tools simply help us reduce the administration burden and free up more time to support quality care. If you would like further information, please let us know.

## Saturday sessions: surprise appointments!

If you’d like to be notified when Saturday appointments are available, opt in to our SMS notification list by popping your name down [here](https://forms.gle/jxFKrfqM7K6sCzsw5).
