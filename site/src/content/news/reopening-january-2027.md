- **Tuesday:** 8am – 8pm
- **Wednesday:** 6am – 8pm

- **Friday:** Closed
- **Saturday:** By appointment only

Small group classes are back, all with Nicole. See the [new class timetable](/pilates#timetable) for all the times.

### Book in now

Bookings for January are open now for both one-on-one appointments and classes so you can [book online](https://book.nookal.com/bookings/book/EE4e4bFE-aEC6-E51D-2cda-aE95ddcBeb3E/location). We expect many of our classes to have a waitlist, so recommend booking early! If you want to lock in a regular booking time, text Nicole on [0434 686 261](sms:+61434686261).

Vestibular physiotherapy will be available again from March, when Laura returns.

## Coming soon: our patient portal

We're excited to launch our new Patient Portal, where you can easily book appointments, pay invoices, securely message clinic staff and update your details online! Keep an eye out for your unique sign-up link in your next appointment reminder to get started. If you’d like to know more about the portal, ask Nicole at your first appointment back.
