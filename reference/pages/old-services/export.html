Our Services

  • Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy

    Conditions we commonly treat:

    • Musculoskeletal and sporting injuries: Sprains, strains, muscle tears, tendonitis, overuse injuries, and more.

    • Pregnancy and postnatal aches and pains: Back pain, pelvic pain, diastasis recti, and post-delivery discomfort.

    • Back and neck pain: From acute stiffness to chronic discomfort, we create self management plans for long term support

    • Joint pain: Whether arthritis, auto-immue, or another issue, we help restore mobility and reduce inflammation.

    • Chronic pain: Our holistic approach manages pain and empowers you to take control of your life.

    • Post surgical care: Support your recovery process after surgery with customized physiotherapy programs.

    Services we offer:

    • Comprehensive assessments: We understand your unique needs and goals to craft the perfect treatment plan.

    • Manual therapy: Massage, joint mobilization, and cupping to address muscle tension and improve joint range of motion.

    • Dry needling: Precisely target trigger points for pain relief and muscle activation.

    • Taping: Support your body and enhance athletic performance or move with less discomfort

    • Personalised exercise programs: Strengthen, stabilize, and improve flexibility with tailored exercises.

    • Education and self-management tools: Empower yourself to maintain optimal health and prevent future issues.

  • Exercise Physiology

    Conditions we commonly treat:

    Injury rehabilitation

    Injury prevention

    Prenatal and Postnatal exercise

    Chronic pain and disease management

    Osteoporosis and healthy ageing

    Musculoskeletal and Neurological rehabilitation

    Strength and Conditioning

    Athletic performance

    What to Expect:

    During your initial consultation, we will take the time to understand your medical history, current fitness level, and specific health concerns. A detailed physical assessment will follow to evaluate your movement patterns, strength, and flexibility. This thorough approach ensures that we develop a safe and effective exercise plan just for you.

    Treatment may include:

    Exercise Guidelines: Personalized advice on how to incorporate exercise into your daily routine safely and effectively.

    Pilates: A focus on core strength, flexibility, and overall body awareness to enhance your physical health.

    Functional and Resistance Training: Exercises designed to improve your strength, endurance, and ability to perform everyday activities with ease.

    Coaching: One-on-one support and motivation to help you achieve your fitness and health goals.

    Home Exercises and Gym Programs: Customized exercise routines that can be performed at home or in the gym, tailored to your specific needs.

    Injury Rehabilitation: Targeted exercises to aid recovery and prevent future injuries.

    Injury Prevention: Strategies and exercises to reduce the risk of future injuries.

    Chronic Pain and Disease Management: Specialized programs to manage conditions like arthritis, diabetes, and cardiovascular disease through exercise.

    Osteoporosis and Healthy Ageing: Exercises to strengthen bones, improve balance, and promote healthy ageing.

    Musculoskeletal and Neurological Rehabilitation: Programs designed to improve movement and function for those with musculoskeletal or neurological conditions.

    Strength and Conditioning & Athletic Performance: Tailored programs to enhance athletic performance and overall physical conditioning.

  • Pelvic Floor Physiotherapy

    Conditions we commonly treat: 

    • Incontinence (urine or bowel)

    • Prolapse

    • Pelvic pain (such as endometriosis, vulvodynia, vaginismus etc )

    • Diastasis recti (abdominal separation)

    • Pre- and postnatal care

    • Sexual dysfunction

    • Menopause care. 

    What to Expect: During your initial consultation your physiotherapist will discuss your medical history and concerns. They will then perform a comprehensive assessment (potentially a vaginal examination if deemed necessary) to determine the best course of treatment.

    Treatment may include:

    • Education about pelvic floor anatomy and function

    • Exercises to strengthen and retrain the pelvic floor muscles

    • Biofeedback to improve muscle awareness

    • Manual therapy

    • Electrical stimulation
