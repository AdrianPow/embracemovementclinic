## Pelvic Floor Physiotherapy

**What to Expect**

During your initial consultation your physiotherapist will discuss your medical history and concerns. They will then perform a comprehensive assessment (potentially a vaginal examination if deemed necessary) to determine the best course of treatment.

**Treatment may include**

- Education about pelvic floor anatomy and function
- Exercises to strengthen and retrain the pelvic floor muscles
- Biofeedback to improve muscle awareness
- Manual therapy
- Electrical stimulation

**Conditions we commonly treat**

- Incontinence (urine or bowel)
- Prolapse
- Pelvic pain (such as endometriosis, vaginismus etc )
- Diastasis recti (abdominal separation)
- Pre- and postnatal care
- Sexual dysfunction
- Menopause care
